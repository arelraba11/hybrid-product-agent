Tidal Soundbar 500 - Product Information Sheet

Tidal Soundbar 500 is a home theater soundbar with a wireless subwoofer for living rooms and medium-sized spaces. It supports Dolby Atmos and connects to a TV with a single HDMI eARC cable.

The Tidal Soundbar 500 costs 429 USD, including the wireless subwoofer. It is a 5.1-channel system with a total output power of 380W.

FAQ Highlights:
- Channels: 5.1 (with wireless subwoofer)
- Total power: 380W
- Dolby Atmos: supported
- Price: 429 USD

Audio System

- Configuration: 5.1 channels (left, center, right, two side-firing surround drivers, and the wireless subwoofer)
- Total output power: 380W (soundbar 220W, subwoofer 160W)
- Drivers in the soundbar: 7 (including a dedicated center channel for clear dialogue)
- Subwoofer: 6.5-inch down-firing driver
- Frequency response: 38Hz to 20kHz
- Supported formats: Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital Plus, DTS:X, PCM

The soundbar creates a virtual height effect for Dolby Atmos using signal processing; it does not have physical up-firing drivers. Adding the optional rear speakers turns the system into 7.1 channels.

Sound Modes

- Movie mode widens the soundstage for films
- Music mode keeps a balanced stereo image
- Night mode reduces loud effects and boosts dialogue so you can watch late at night
- Voice Enhance raises the center channel for news and talk shows
- Automatic mode selects the sound mode based on the content

Connectivity

- HDMI eARC to connect to the TV (cable included)
- HDMI input with 4K HDR passthrough for a game console or streaming box
- Optical input for older TVs
- Wi-Fi with built-in streaming from music apps
- Bluetooth 5.3
- Multi-room audio with other Tidal speakers through the Tidal Home app

The soundbar can be controlled with the TV remote through HDMI CEC, the included remote, or the Tidal Home app.

Dimensions and Placement

- Soundbar: 98 x 6.5 x 11 cm, 3.6kg
- Subwoofer: 20 x 38 x 38 cm, 7.1kg
The soundbar fits in front of TVs from 50 to 75 inches and can be wall-mounted with the included bracket. The subwoofer connects wirelessly and only needs a power outlet; place it within 10 meters of the soundbar.

Power Use

The soundbar uses about 45W during normal listening and less than 2W in network standby. The subwoofer uses about 30W during normal listening.

Setup

Setup takes about 10 minutes. Connect the soundbar to the TV's HDMI eARC port, plug in the subwoofer, and follow the Tidal Home app to connect to Wi-Fi. The app runs a room calibration that uses the phone microphone to adjust the sound to your room.

Warranty and Support

The Tidal Soundbar 500 has a 2-year limited warranty on the soundbar and subwoofer. The remote control is covered for 1 year. Firmware updates that add features are delivered automatically over Wi-Fi.
