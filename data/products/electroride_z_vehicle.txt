ElectroRide Z - Electric Vehicle Fact Sheet

ElectroRide Z is a compact electric vehicle for urban driving. It uses a 62kWh battery pack and a single-motor rear-wheel drive architecture.

Estimated range in mixed city/highway driving is 410 km per full charge. DC fast charging from 20% to 80% takes around 32 minutes under ideal conditions.

Infotainment includes a 14-inch center screen, wireless phone integration, and built-in navigation. Safety package includes lane assist, adaptive cruise control, and emergency braking.

Base model launch price is 32900 USD.

Powertrain and Performance

- Motor: single permanent-magnet motor, rear-wheel drive
- Power: 150kW (about 204 horsepower)
- Torque: 310Nm
- Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 7.4 seconds
- Top speed: 160 km/h
- Regenerative braking: three levels plus one-pedal driving mode

Battery, Range and Charging

The ElectroRide Z uses a 62kWh battery pack (usable capacity 59kWh). The estimated range in mixed city/highway driving is 410 km per full charge. This is the official range figure. Other range values depend on conditions:
- City-only driving in mild weather: up to 480 km
- Highway driving at 120 km/h: about 300 km
- Winter driving at -10 degrees Celsius: about 290 km

DC fast charging from 20% to 80% takes around 32 minutes under ideal conditions, with a peak charging power of 130kW. AC charging at 11kW takes about 6.5 hours from 0 to 100 percent. A heat pump is standard and helps preserve range in cold weather.

Trims and Pricing

- Base model (launch price): 32,900 USD
- Long Range trim with an 82kWh battery and an estimated 540 km range: 38,500 USD
- Optional Comfort Package (heated seats, heated steering wheel, premium audio): 1,800 USD
- Home wallbox charger with standard installation: 1,100 USD
Prices exclude taxes, registration and delivery fees. Government incentives may lower the final price depending on the country.

Dimensions and Practicality

- Length: 4,150 mm; width: 1,800 mm; height: 1,560 mm
- Wheelbase: 2,700 mm
- Seating: 5 adults
- Cargo space: 380 liters, up to 1,150 liters with rear seats folded
- Front trunk: 35 liters, suitable for charging cables
- Turning circle: 10.2 m
- Curb weight: 1,720 kg

Infotainment and Connectivity

Infotainment includes a 14-inch center screen, wireless phone integration, and built-in navigation with charging station routing. A 10-inch digital driver display shows speed, range and navigation directions. Over-the-air software updates are included for 8 years. The companion app allows remote climate control, charging schedules and locking.

Safety

The safety package includes lane assist, adaptive cruise control, and emergency braking. Additional standard features are blind spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, a 360-degree camera and seven airbags. The ElectroRide Z received a five-star rating in independent crash testing.

Warranty and Service

- Vehicle warranty: 4 years or 100,000 km, whichever comes first
- Battery and drive unit warranty: 8 years or 160,000 km, guaranteeing at least 70 percent battery capacity
- Recommended service interval: every 2 years or 30,000 km
- Roadside assistance: included for 4 years
