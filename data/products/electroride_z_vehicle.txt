ElectroRide Z - Electric Vehicle Fact Sheet

ElectroRide Z is a compact electric vehicle for urban driving. It uses a 62kWh battery pack and a single-motor rear-wheel drive architecture.

Estimated range in mixed city/highway driving is 410 km per full charge. DC fast charging from 20% to 80% takes around 32 minutes under ideal conditions.

Infotainment includes a 14-inch center screen, wireless phone integration, and built-in navigation. Safety package includes lane assist, adaptive cruise control, and emergency braking.

Base model launch price is 32900 USD.
