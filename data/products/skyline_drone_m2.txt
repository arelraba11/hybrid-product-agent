Skyline Drone M2 - Product Specification and Buyer Guide

Skyline Drone M2 is a foldable camera drone for travel photographers and content creators. It weighs under 250 grams, which means it does not need registration in many countries for recreational use.

The standard Skyline Drone M2 package costs 689 USD. It records 4K video at 60 frames per second, has a maximum flight time of 34 minutes per battery, and a video transmission range of up to 10 km.

Aircraft Specifications

- Takeoff weight: 249 grams
- Folded size: 148 x 90 x 62 mm
- Maximum horizontal speed: 57 km/h (Sport mode)
- Maximum wind resistance: 38 km/h (Level 5)
- Maximum service ceiling: 4,000 m above sea level
- Operating temperature: -10 to 40 degrees Celsius
- Satellite positioning: GPS, Galileo and BeiDou
- Internal storage: 2GB, plus a microSD slot up to 256GB

Camera and Gimbal

The camera uses a 1/1.3-inch 48MP sensor with an f/1.7 aperture. Video recording supports 4K at up to 60 frames per second, 2.7K at 60fps and 1080p slow motion at 120fps. A 10-bit color profile is available for color grading. The 3-axis mechanical gimbal stabilizes footage and can rotate 90 degrees for vertical shots for social media. Photo modes include single shot, burst, panorama, and automatic exposure bracketing.

Flight Time and Batteries

The Skyline Drone M2 Intelligent Flight Battery has a capacity of 2,450mAh. The maximum flight time is 34 minutes per battery, measured while hovering in calm conditions with no wind. This is the official flight time figure. Realistic values are lower:
- Typical flight time with normal filming and moderate wind: about 26 minutes
- Flight time with the optional Plus battery: up to 45 minutes (the Plus battery raises takeoff weight above 250 grams)

A battery charges from 0 to 100 percent in about 70 minutes with a 30W USB-C charger. The two-way charging hub charges three batteries in sequence.

Transmission and Controller

Video transmission range is up to 10 km in open areas without interference (FCC). In urban areas, expect 1.5 to 3 km. Live view is streamed at 1080p 30fps to the RC-M controller, which holds a smartphone. The controller battery lasts about 4 hours.

Safety Features

- Obstacle sensing on the front, rear and bottom
- Automatic return to home when the battery is low or the signal is lost
- Geofencing warnings near airports and restricted zones
- Precision landing using the downward vision system

Packages and Pricing

Regulations

Because it weighs 249 grams, the Skyline Drone M2 falls under the lightest drone category in many countries. Pilots must still follow local rules: keep the drone within visual line of sight, do not fly over crowds, and respect no-fly zones. Rules differ by country, so check local regulations before flying.

Warranty and Support

The Skyline Drone M2 includes a 1-year limited warranty on the aircraft and a 6-month warranty on batteries. Crash damage is not covered by the standard warranty. The SkyCare plan, priced at 79 USD per year, covers up to two replacements for crash or water damage.
