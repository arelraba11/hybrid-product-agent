BrewMaster Y - Coffee Machine Manual Extract

BrewMaster Y is an automatic coffee machine aimed at office and home users who need quick drink preparation. It supports espresso, lungo, and cappuccino profiles with a one-touch panel.

Water tank capacity is 1.8 liters. Bean hopper supports 250 grams. Milk frother module is detachable for easy cleaning.

Average brew cycle is 38 seconds for espresso and 52 seconds for cappuccino. Eco mode powers down the heater after 15 minutes of inactivity.

Suggested retail price is 349 USD.

Drink Profiles

BrewMaster Y has three one-touch drink profiles: espresso, lungo and cappuccino. Each profile can be adjusted for strength (five levels), cup volume and temperature (three levels). Two personal profiles can be saved on the panel. The average brew cycle is 38 seconds for espresso, 45 seconds for lungo and 52 seconds for cappuccino. A double-shot button brews two espressos in about 70 seconds.

Milk System

The detachable milk frother module connects to a milk carton or the optional milk jug with a flexible tube. It produces milk foam for cappuccino and can also dispense hot milk. The frother can be removed with one click and is dishwasher safe. An automatic rinse runs after every milk drink.

Energy Use and Eco Mode

Eco mode powers down the heater after 15 minutes of inactivity; the delay can be changed to 30 or 60 minutes. In standby the machine uses less than 0.5W. The machine meets the A+ energy efficiency class for coffee machines.

Cleaning and Maintenance

- Empty the grounds container when the panel shows the container icon (about every 12 drinks).
- Rinse the brewing unit weekly under running water; it can be removed from the side door.
- Descale every 2-3 months, or when the descale light turns on. With a water filter installed, descaling is needed about every 6 months.
- Replace the water filter cartridge every 2 months.

Troubleshooting

- Coffee is too weak: choose a finer grind setting and a higher strength level.
- Machine does not start brewing: check that the water tank and the grounds container are fully inserted.
- Milk does not foam: rinse the frother tube and check that the milk is cold.
