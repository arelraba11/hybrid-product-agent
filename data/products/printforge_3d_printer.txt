Overview of the Printer

The PrintForge 3D Printer is a fused-filament printer with an open frame, designed so students can see each part of the printing process. Assembly takes about 30 minutes with the included tools. It weighs 6.5kg and its footprint is 42x40cm, so it fits on a standard classroom desk.

Specifications

- Printing technology: fused filament fabrication (FFF)
- Build volume: 220x220x250mm
- Layer height: 0.1-0.3mm
- Print speed: 40-80 mm/s depending on quality settings
- Nozzle diameter: 0.4mm (brass)
- Filament diameter: 1.75mm
- Supported material: PLA only (ABS and PETG are not supported because the hotend is limited to 220 degrees Celsius)
- Heated bed: up to 60 degrees Celsius, removable magnetic plate
- Connectivity: USB transfer cable and microSD card
- Display: 2.8-inch color touch screen
- Noise level: about 48dB during printing

Power and Maintenance

Power use averages 120W during active print sessions, with a peak of about 250W while the bed heats up. In standby the printer draws less than 5W. Typical nozzle replacement interval is every 4-6 months under moderate usage. The build plate should be cleaned with isopropyl alcohol every few prints, and the guide rods should be lubricated about once a month in heavy classroom use.

Software

PrintForge works with the free PrintForge Slicer for Windows, macOS and Linux, and with common open-source slicers using the provided printer profile. Lesson plans for introductory 3D design are available on the PrintForge education portal.

Safety

The hotend reaches 220 degrees Celsius; students should be supervised. The printer has thermal runaway protection that cuts power to the heater if the temperature rises unexpectedly. PLA is a plant-based material and is the recommended filament for classrooms.
