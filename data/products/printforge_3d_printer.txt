PrintForge 3D Printer - User Notes and Pricing

PrintForge is a compact educational 3D printer intended for school labs and home makers. It uses PLA filament, supports 0.1-0.3mm layer heights, and has a printable area of 220x220x250mm.

The base starter package includes printer body, sample filament, and USB transfer cable. Printing speed ranges between 40 and 80 mm/s depending on quality settings.

Power use averages 120W during active print sessions. Typical nozzle replacement interval is every 4-6 months under moderate usage.

Current promotional store price for the base package is 25 USD.
